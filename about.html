Our Story

How it started: Kevina Creations is a home-based and online business that started in October 2014. After an insight, and seeing examples of individuals globally running successful home-based businesses, we decided to start.


We initially had physical premises at Nsambya craft center, where the business transformed and grew with major customers originating from family circles, art exhibitions, and referrals. However, due to COVID-19 effects, we transitioned into an online and home-based business in 2021.


Our Name: The business has gone through a series of name changes such as "Gifted Figures" and "Kevina Crafts" before becoming "Kevina Creations". The business was named after a profound lady, the Late Kevina Kamanyi; a wife, a mother, a grandmother, and a friend to many (RIP 2016). She was the first and most frequent client for this business.


Our Goal: We aim to admire and appreciate handmade African items specifically made from Uganda. Through our products, we hope to bring out African beauty and creativity at its best, marketing and promoting our country nationally and globally. We encourage our client base to appreciate art as a talent, a source of income, and a source of identity.


Customized Products: Orders for customized products are accepted. These are made according to the client's specifications, with ample time for production and delivery after subsequent discussions.
